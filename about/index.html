One Step Ahead MMA & Fitness

Serious coaching
Good people

A Bristol gym where complete beginners, regular members and experienced fighters can learn together and take their training seriously.

OSA is an independent MMA and fitness gym in Clifton, led by Bristol fighter and coach Nad Narimani.

What the club stands for

Good coaching should be clear, practical and open to people who are ready to learn. OSA combines high-level knowledge with a welcoming club atmosphere, giving every member room to improve at their own pace.

Complete MMA under one roof

The weekly programme covers MMA, striking, NoGi grappling, wrestling and kids classes. Members can follow the full programme or focus on the disciplines that match their interests.

How we train together

Respect your coaches and training partners. Arrive with clean kit, clipped nails and no jewellery. Keep outdoor shoes off the mats. Discrimination, bullying and harassment have no place at OSA.

At a glance

  • All experience levels welcome
  • Adult and kids sessions
  • Led by UFC veteran Nad Narimani
  • Opposite Clifton Down station
