Which option should I choose?

Start with a drop-in if you want to experience a class before deciding. Unlimited membership is the straightforward option when you plan to train regularly across the adult timetable.

Can I train in more than one discipline?

Yes. Unlimited adult membership covers the scheduled adult programme, so you can combine MMA, striking, NoGi and wrestling.
