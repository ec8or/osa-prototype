1-to-1 coaching

Train one-to-one with Nad.

Choose a private session for concentrated work on MMA, grappling, striking, fitness or a specific technical goal.

Private coaching gives you more time on the details that matter to you, with the session adapted to your experience and priorities.

Who this class is for

Private coaching works for beginners who want a confident introduction, active members solving a particular problem and small groups who want to train together.

What to expect

Tell OSA what you want from the session when you enquire. Your coach can then shape the hour around a clear objective and recommend useful follow-up work.

How it fits your week

Private sessions can stand alone or complement the weekly timetable. Regular group classes give you more partners and opportunities to apply what you learn.

What you’ll work on

  • MMA, striking, wrestling or NoGi technique
  • Fitness and conditioning for your goals
  • One-to-one or small-group sessions
