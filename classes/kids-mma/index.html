Kids MMA

Confidence starts here

A welcoming martial arts programme that helps children build movement, confidence, listening skills and respect for training partners.

Kids learn age-appropriate striking, grappling and wrestling skills in sessions built around safe practice and steady progress.

Who this class is for

The programme is for children and teenagers ready to take part in a coached group class. Contact the gym with your child’s age and experience so the team can recommend the right first session.

What to expect

The coach sets clear rules, explains each activity and keeps the group moving. Children practise with suitable partners and learn that good martial arts training depends on control and respect.

How it fits your week

Kids sessions run across MMA, NoGi, striking and wrestling, giving young members variety while reinforcing the same core habits each week.

What you’ll work on

  • Movement, balance and coordination
  • Clear technical games and partner drills
  • Confidence, focus and respectful behaviour
