Six ways to train.

For most newcomers, mixed MMA is the best place to start. You can then add striking, NoGi or wrestling as you discover what you enjoy.

01

The complete game.

MMA

MMA classes develop the decisions and transitions that happen when a fight moves between standing, clinch and ground positions.
↗02

Build better striking.

Striking

Work on balance, range, defence and combinations while learning how striking changes when takedowns and clinch work are part of the picture.
↗03

Better positions. Better decisions.

NoGi Grappling

OSA combines NoGi Brazilian jiu-jitsu and catch-wrestling influences into a practical grappling programme led by BJJ black belt Nad Narimani.
↗04

Decide where the fight happens.

Wrestling

Learn reliable entries, finishes, defence and mat control, then apply them through purposeful drills and rounds.
↗05

Confidence starts here.

Kids MMA

Kids learn age-appropriate striking, grappling and wrestling skills in sessions built around safe practice and steady progress.
↗06

Train one-to-one with Nad.

Personal Training

Private coaching gives you more time on the details that matter to you, with the session adapted to your experience and priorities.

New to martial arts?

Start in the mixed class.

You don’t need a separate beginner class or a base level of fitness. The coach will pair you appropriately, adjust the work to your experience and help you learn the complete game from the start.
