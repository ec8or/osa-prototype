New to MMA?

Start in the
mixed class.

OSA doesn’t separate new starters into a beginners-only class. You learn in the regular coached sessions, with the work matched to your experience.

Beginners and experienced members train together at OSA because mixed sessions give you better partners, clearer examples and a more complete understanding of MMA from the start.

Why mixed training works

Your coach knows who is new, pairs people appropriately and controls the task and intensity. You can learn from more experienced partners without being expected to keep up with everything they can already do.

What your first class looks like

Expect clear technical teaching, partner drills and controlled practice. Tell the coach it’s your first session, ask questions when you need to and work at the pace set for you. You won’t be thrown into hard sparring.

Where to start

Adult MMA is the best first choice for most people because it introduces striking, takedowns and grappling together. If you’re especially drawn to stand-up or ground work, striking and NoGi are also open to beginners.

Your first session

  • Tell the coach you’re new when you arrive
  • Wear comfortable sports clothing without zips
  • Bring water and arrive a little early
