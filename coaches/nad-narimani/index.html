Head coach · OSA Bristol

Nad
Narimani

A UFC veteran, former Cage Warriors featherweight champion and BJJ black belt teaching proven MMA in his home city.

Nad Narimani built his career through British and international MMA before bringing that experience back to Bristol as the owner and head coach of One Step Ahead MMA & Fitness.

Competitive experience

Nad won the Cage Warriors featherweight title in 2017 and went on to compete in the UFC. His professional record includes victories by knockout, submission and decision—the breadth of experience behind OSA’s complete approach to MMA.

Coaching at OSA

Nad teaches the techniques, tactics and training habits he has tested at the highest level. Classes remain practical and demanding, but the environment is built for learning: clear instruction, suitable partners and steady progression.

Why he created the gym

“I created OSA to give Bristol’s next generation a place to learn proven MMA in a friendly, hard-working environment.”
