Your information

policy

This page explains what information OSA receives when you use the website or contact the gym, and how it is handled.

Last updated: 15 August 2026

Who is responsible for your information

One Step Ahead MMA & Fitness (OSA) is responsible for personal information collected through this website and through direct enquiries about training at the gym.

Information we collect

When you contact OSA, we may receive your name, contact details, the class you are interested in and any information you include in your message. Basic technical information may also be recorded by the website hosting service to keep the site secure and understand how it is used.

How we use it

OSA uses enquiry information to reply, recommend a suitable class, arrange your visit and provide information you have asked for. Technical information is used for security, reliability and aggregated website measurement.

Our lawful reasons

Enquiry details are processed because you have asked OSA to take steps before you attend or join, and because the gym has a legitimate interest in responding to genuine enquiries and operating a secure website. Consent will be requested separately before sending optional marketing messages.

Who receives it

Information is available only to people who need it to run OSA and respond to you. Service providers may process limited information for website hosting, security, messaging or email delivery under their own security and data-protection obligations. OSA does not sell personal information.

How long it is kept

Enquiries are kept only for as long as needed to respond and manage the resulting customer relationship, meet legal obligations or resolve a dispute. Information that is no longer needed is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

You may ask for access to your personal information, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy in a portable format where the relevant right applies. You may also object to certain uses or withdraw consent at any time where processing relies on consent.

Cookies and external services

The public information pages do not need advertising cookies. Links to services such as WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or Google Maps take you to third-party websites whose privacy practices apply once you leave this site.

Contact and complaints

Contact OSA using the phone or WhatsApp details on the contact page with a privacy question or request. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, enquiry process or legal requirements change. The date above shows the latest revision.
